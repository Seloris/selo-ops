# App

The shell of Selo Ops: its address, its header, its theme, its language. The interface is in French.

## Functional requirements

- **FR-01** — The home address (`/`) opens « Sessions live », with no path to add.
- **FR-02** — The header offers a Clair / Sombre theme switch, Sombre by default, remembered in the browser.
- **FR-03** — Every page shows the same header: the « SELO OPS » wordmark, the tabs « Live » (Sessions live, the default view), « Projets » and « Debug », a link « Aide » to the help page and the theme switch; the current page's tab or link is highlighted, and « Projets » and « Debug » are dimmed, marked « Bientôt » and lead nowhere until their pages exist.
