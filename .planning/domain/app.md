# App

The shell of Selo Ops: its address, its header, its theme, its language. The interface is in French.

## Functional requirements

- **FR-01** — The home address (`/`) opens « Sessions live », with no path to add.
- **FR-02** — The header offers a Clair / Sombre theme switch, Sombre by default, remembered in the browser.
- **FR-03** — Every page shows the same header (hidden only in the zen mode of Sessions live, `sessions-live` FR-58): the « SELO OPS » wordmark, the tabs « Live » (Sessions live, the default view), « Projets », « Stats » and « Debug », « Aide », which opens the help in a popup (`help` FR-10), and the theme switch; the current page's tab is highlighted (on the help page, « Aide » is its highlighted link), « Projets » opens the projects page, « Stats » the stats page, and « Debug » is dimmed, marked « Bientôt » and leads nowhere until its page exists.
- **FR-04** — A page spec, a domain sheet (the `app` sheet with them) and a blueprint each wear one colour and one icon, the same wherever one shows (the projects page, the help's « Specs » tab): page indigo with a window, domain sheet pink with a book, blueprint teal (the blueprint lane's colour) with a plan.
- **FR-05** — A bell in the header, before « Réglages » (FR-08), opens a small panel to choose the states that notify: « Attend ta réponse », « Attend ta permission », « Commit à valider » and « Arrêtée » (those « à traiter », `session` BR-04), then « Tour terminé » and « Job done », none checked by default, the choice remembered in the browser; checking the first one asks the browser's permission, and a refused permission shows « Notifications bloquées par le navigateur » in the panel instead of the states; the bell shows the count of states that notify in a small badge, and is struck through while none does, and left out where the browser has no notifications. Whatever the page and while the app is open, even in the background, an open session that enters a checked state, or moves from one to another, raises a browser notification naming its state and its project, with its request; the sessions already in a checked state when the app opens or when the choice changes raise none, nor does a session while the app's window has the focus; a click on the notification brings the app to the front on the session's detail (`sessions-live` FR-18).
- **FR-07** — Whatever the page and while the app is open, even in the background, a flagged session with its « Me notifier » switch on (`sessions-live` FR-42) that enters « Tour terminé » or « Job done » raises the same notification as FR-05, whatever states the bell checks, and one notification only when the bell checks that state too; turning the switch on raises nothing for a session already in one of them, nor does a session while the app's window has the focus.
- **FR-08** — « Réglages » in the header, between the bell and the theme switch, opens a window, full screen on a phone, read from the file each time it opens (`settings` BR-01): the file's path; « Modèles des agents »: the preset « Standard » / « Lean » with what each sets, then one line per role (« Planifier », « Implémenter », « Relire », « Exécuter », « Chercher ») with its role, its agent and a menu « Selon le preset ({model}) », opus, sonnet, haiku, fable (`settings` BR-02); « Pendant le travail »: the switches « Router mes demandes vers pilot » and « Commiter sans me demander », each with its key and value; then « S'applique aux sessions ouvertes après l'enregistrement. ».
- **FR-09** — A role set by hand carries « personnalisé » and an arrow back to « Selon le preset »; a model the menu does not offer shows as is, as the role's choice.
- **FR-10** — Once a setting changed, the window shows « Écrit dans env à l'enregistrement » with the `SELO_` keys `env` will hold, and the count of unsaved changes; « Enregistrer » stays disabled until a change; « Annuler », « Fermer » and Échap close without writing and without asking.
- **FR-11** — « Enregistrer » saves (`settings` BR-03), closes the window and shows the toast « Réglages enregistrés · pour les prochaines sessions »; a failed write keeps the window open, its error in a frame above the buttons.
- **FR-12** — An unreadable file (`settings` BR-04) shows « Impossible de lire tes réglages. » with the file's path, the line and column of the error and « Corrige le fichier, puis rouvre cette fenêtre. », the settings disabled, « Enregistrer » disabled and « Annuler » reading « Fermer ».
- **FR-06** — The browser can install Selo Ops as an app of its own, named « Selo Ops », opening on « Sessions live » in its own window with the app's icon.
