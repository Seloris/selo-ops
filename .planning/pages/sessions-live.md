# Sessions live

Route: `/` · Domain: `session`, `app`

The home page of Selo Ops: every Claude Code session of the selo projects, live, so that the person sees at a glance which ones wait on them. It reads the ops export and acts on no session (`session` BR-01).

## Header

- **FR-01** — The subtitle counts the open sessions that are not archived, their projects and those « à traiter » (`session` BR-04), whatever the project filter and the search.
- **FR-02** — The page refreshes itself every 5 s and shows « Mis à jour il y a X s », preceded by the versions of the selo plugins Claude Code lists as installed (`session` BR-01; « selo 2.2.0 · selo-ops 2.2.1 », every version of a plugin installed at several, highest first: « selo 2.2.0 / 2.1.0 »), left out when that list is missing or unreadable.
- **FR-43** — « Nouvelle session » closes the header's line (below 640 px, its « + » icon alone, named on hover) and opens the « Nouvelle session » window (FR-44) on the project of the most recently updated open session, else on the first project.

## Settings bar

- **FR-03** — « Affichage » switches between Liste (one two-line row per session) and Cartes (a grid of cards with every detail), Liste by default.
- **FR-04** — « Regrouper » offers Par projet (one block per project), Par statut (À traiter, En cours, Au repos, Job done) and Aucun (one list), Par projet by default.
- **FR-05** — « Projets » is a checklist of the projects with « Tous », each with its count of open sessions, all checked by default.
- **FR-06** — « Afficher les fermées », unchecked by default, with its count, adds a « Fermées » block of the closed sessions not archived (`session` BR-03).
- **FR-07** — The search filters on the request, the blueprint, the branch and the project, in every block.
- **FR-08** — The view, the grouping and the project filter (as the unchecked projects, so a new project shows) are remembered in the browser; « Afficher les fermées » and the search are not.

## Sessions

- **FR-09** — Sessions sort by state (`session` BR-02), then the most recently updated first; by project, the project holding the most urgent session comes first.
- **FR-39** — A session written by a selo-ops version that is not one the header lists shows a small amber warning, « Écrite par selo-ops 2.1.0, autre version que celle installée », beside its state in the list and the cards and beside « Écrit par » in the detail; nothing shows when the export gives no version or Claude Code lists no selo-ops.
- **FR-10** — A state has one colour, the same in the row, the card and the detail: amber for « Attend ta réponse », « Attend ta permission » and « Commit à valider », rose for « Arrêtée » (their rows tinted with it), a pulsing blue dot for « En cours » (a blue no type of work and no other control uses), a green dot for « Job done » and the same green as a hollow ring for « Tour terminé », a hollow grey one for a closed or archived session, whatever its state; in the list, violet marks only the « Action suivante » and the pilot type of work (FR-29). The state shows as a badge: its dot and label on a tint of its colour, lighter for « Tour terminé », grey for a closed or archived session.
- **FR-11** — A row holds two lines. The first shows the state's badge, the request on the rest of the line (cut with « … » at its end, whole on hover) and the age at the end. The second shows, under the badge, the type of work (`session` BR-22) and the project unless grouped by project (« fastlane · selo-ops »), whole, the badge column as wide as the list's widest; then the context: what is awaited when the session is « à traiter » (its icon and text in the state's colour), else the step of a running session (« Étape 2 · tests du web »), else the « Action suivante » as a violet chip with its command (FR-21), else « Rien d'attendu »; then the count of files the session modified (a file icon and the number, « 3 fichiers modifiés » on hover; none at 0 or for a session outside selo) and the session's total tokens (`session` BR-18, abbreviated as in FR-35; none when the export gives no usage). The « Action suivante » (when the session has one, FR-21), « Marquer comme importante » (FR-42), « Note » and « Ouvrir dans Claude Code » icon buttons close the row, across both lines; a session with a note (`session` BR-21) has its « Note » button in blue, and hovering or focusing it shows the whole note in a small card under the button. The list has no column header. Below 900 px of width, the request takes its own line under the badge and the age, and the line after it keeps only the context.
- **FR-29** — The type of work reads as monospace text in its lane's colour, the help's (help FR-11), in the row, the card and the detail: « libre » slate, « fastlane » lime, « blueprint » teal, « pilot » and « pilot autonome » violet; a mockup (« libre › mockup ») fuchsia and a fastlane's iterate loop (« fastlane › itération », `session` BR-22) orange, the help's colours for them (help FR-11); « hors selo » stays neutral.
- **FR-12** — A project block's header shows its name, its branch, its count of uncommitted files (none for a project only sessions outside selo show), its count « à traiter » and its count of sessions, then, unless only sessions outside selo show the project, a « + » icon button, « Nouvelle session dans {projet} » on hover, that opens the « Nouvelle session » window (FR-44) on that project.
- **FR-13** — A card adds the whole request with the whole note under it, what is awaited (or what the session does when nothing is), the model (`session` BR-20) with the session's total tokens (the four kinds summed, `session` BR-18) and effort, the permission mode, whether it can be closed, the « Me notifier » switch of a flagged session (FR-42), and the « Ouvrir », « Action suivante », « Note », « Marquer comme importante » and « Archiver » buttons.
- **FR-30** — Wherever a session outside selo (`session` BR-12) shows, its effort, permissions, total tokens and usage (`session` BR-18) read « — » and its recap, commits, subagents, files modified and blueprint are left out, since only the export gives them.
- **FR-14** — The age is the time since the session's last update; a row whose state is Job done or Tour terminé reads « il y a {âge} »; a closed row shows « Fermée · {âge} », an archived one « Archivée · {âge} ».
- **FR-15** — What is awaited is the question (« Claude attend ta réponse dans la session » when its text is unknown: a session outside selo, or a question the export missed, `session` BR-17), the tool to allow (« Autoriser : {commande} » for a shell command, else « Autoriser : {outil} », « Claude attend ta permission dans la session » when the transcript names none), the proposed commit's subject or the error; an « Arrêtée » session with no error shows « Interrompue, sans message d'erreur ».
- **FR-24** — A request of which the export holds no readable text shows « Demande non lisible dans l'export », or « Image seule, sans texte » when its prompt is an image alone (`session` BR-10).
- **FR-16** — An empty list says why: no project checked, no session matching the search, or no open session.
- **FR-42** — « Marquer comme importante », an icon button on every session (row, card and detail), flags the session: its flag fills and turns yellow on a yellow pill, the button then reading « Retirer le marqueur important »; a flag neither groups, sorts nor filters. A flagged session offers, in its card and its detail, a « Me notifier à la fin du tour et du job » switch, off by default (app FR-07); turning it on asks the browser's permission first, a refused permission leaves it off and disabled with « Notifications bloquées par le navigateur », and it is left out where the browser has no notifications; removing the flag turns it off. The flags and the switches are remembered in the browser, not on the server.
- **FR-17** — « Archivées » is a folded section at the bottom with its count; opened, it lists the archived sessions in the current view.
- **FR-25** — When a refresh fails after a first load, the list stays as last read, under a message saying the refresh failed.

## Detail

- **FR-18** — A click on a session opens its detail in a panel sliding from the right; the list stays visible, its badge column narrower; the address keeps the open session, and Échap or the icon-only close button, named « Fermer » on hover, closes it.
- **FR-37** — Above 900 px of width, an icon-only full-screen button, beside the close button in the detail, puts the detail alone on the screen: the browser goes full screen, the app's header, the page's header and settings bar and the list are hidden and the grip bar is gone; the button then shows the reduce icon and gives everything back with the chosen width, as does Échap (a second Échap closes the detail); closing the detail leaves the full screen; it is not remembered.
- **FR-27** — Above 900 px of width, a vertical grip bar, always visible and centred in the gap between the list and the detail, drags (or moves with the arrow keys once focused) to share the width between them, the detail at least 360 px wide and the list at least 560 px; the chosen width is remembered in the browser, and a double click on the edge goes back to the default width. Below 900 px the detail covers the screen and has no edge.
- **FR-19** — The detail shows the state and tag, the request, the whole note under it, project, branch, type, the model and effort when known, the day and time the session was created (`session` BR-15; « créée le 22 oct. à 17:41 », with the year when it is not the current one) and age; the whole awaited text (a commit with its body); « Peut-on fermer la session ? » with « Oui, rien ne bloque » or « Pas encore » and its blockers (`session` BR-06), or « Inconnu hors selo » with no blocker for a session outside selo; the recap and commits of a finished run; the subagents of the current run (type, task, state, real model and total tokens, `session` BR-20, BR-18); « Fichiers modifiés », the files the session modified as a tree of folders, each file with an icon of its type (Markdown, TypeScript, React, JavaScript, JSON, styles, HTML, YAML, image, script, text; a plain file for any other extension); the model (`session` BR-20, « — » when unknown), effort, permissions, blueprint, folder, session id and « Écrit par », the version of the selo-ops plugin that wrote the session's export file (« selo-ops 2.2.1 », « — » outside selo or in an export that gives none).
- **FR-28** — « Pas encore » comes with the count of blockers (« 2 choses bloquent la fermeture »), then one line per blocker, in the export's order: its label and what clears it — « Le travail n’a pas fini par JOB DONE : laisse-le aboutir ou relance-le. », « Un commit attend ton accord : valide-le ou refuse-le dans la session. », « Claude attend ta réponse dans la session. », « Des fichiers ne sont pas commités : commite-les ou annule-les. », « Une tâche de fond tourne encore : attends sa fin ou arrête-la. », « Des sous-agents travaillent encore : attends qu’ils finissent. »; « Commit en attente » hides « Travail pas terminé » and « Fichiers modifiés », which it implies, here and in the cards' « whether it can be closed »; the dot of a question or a commit to approve is amber, since it waits on the person, that of a background task or subagents pulses, the others are grey.
- **FR-51** — A click on a file of « Fichiers modifiés » opens its diff under the tree (a second click, or its close button « Fermer le diff », closes it), read from git when asked and never refreshed every 5 s (`session` BR-01): one part per commit the export recorded for the session's runs that changes the file, oldest first, with its short hash and subject, then « Non commité », the file against the last commit (a file git does not track yet in full), with « Sur un checkout partagé, cette partie peut aussi contenir les modifications d'autres sessions. »; added lines read in green, removed lines in red; a commit git no longer has (a worktree landed, then removed) reads « Diff indisponible : ce commit n'existe plus. », a session folder that is gone or holds no git repository « Diff indisponible : le dossier de la session n'existe plus ou n'est pas un dépôt git. », a file with no part « Aucun changement trouvé pour ce fichier. », and a part longer than 200 000 characters is cut with « Diff tronqué ».
- **FR-41** — « Déroulé du tour », in the detail, lists the current run's history as the export keeps it, newest first, as a vertical timeline: one line per change with its time to the second (« 21:39:12 »), a dot in its state's colour and the skill (fastlane, blueprint and build, pilot in their lane's colour, mockup in its flow's, FR-29, the lines of a fastlane's iterate loop (`session` BR-22) in the iterate flow's orange; the others neutral), the step (« Étape 2 ») and substep, the state's badge (its label alone, the dot being the timeline's); a past « En cours » line, finished once a line follows it, reads in grey with no badge; each subagent of the current run (FR-19) is a line of its own at the time it started, with a dot in its state's colour, its task (« planner · blueprint selo-settings », the word after « · » in its lane's colour when it names a lane skill, as above, else neutral), how long it ran (until the export last saw it, until now while it works) and its state's badge, so that a pilot's steps show beside its own lines; a line joins each dot to the one below, the earlier line, in the colour of the state the run was in then (dashed for a wait on the person), longer as the time between them was longer (square root of its duration, bounded, so that a few seconds stay visible beside an hour), with that time written beside it, halfway between the two dots; only the dots of the run's newest line while it runs and of the subagents still working pulse; folded, the heading shows the run as a ribbon, oldest on the left, one segment per line of the run, subagents left out, in its state's colour, its width growing with the square root of its duration, the last line counting until now; both are bounded so that a line ended long ago does not swallow the others; its heading sums the time spent in « Attend ta réponse » and « Commit à valider » (« 6 min à t'attendre »); when the export dropped the oldest entries a last line reads « Entrées plus anciennes non gardées par l'export »; the section is left out for a session outside selo and for a run with no history.
- **FR-32** — « Consommation » opens on the whole session (`session` BR-18), read top-down. A summary card shows the total tokens in large with « tokens consommés » and, when one model made them all, its name; beside it the API calls, « Cache réutilisé » (the part of what the model read that came from the cache) and « Sortie », each hint in its tooltip. « Répartition des tokens » gives one row per kind (« Entrée », « Cache lu », « Cache écrit », « Sortie », each in its own colour): its name and what it is, a bar of its share of the total, its count and its share; in the side panel the explanations move to the tooltips. « Modèles » shows only when several models share the session: one row per model, the most tokens first, with its bar, tokens, share of the session and calls.
- **FR-36** — « Prompts », with its count, lists the main runs the export keeps, the latest first, since a free prompt opens a run of its own; a single run whose tokens are the session's is not listed (it would repeat the summary), unless the list is cut. A single run with no figures at all reads « Pas de consommation dans l’export pour ce tour. ». Each line gives its time (with the day when it is not today, and the year when it is not the current one), its request cut to one line, whole in its tooltip (« Demande non lisible dans l’export » when unreadable), a bar of its tokens scaled to the biggest run (hidden in the side panel), its tokens and its share of the session (« — » for a run with no usage, `session` BR-18); a click unfolds the run: its calls and sole model, a bar splitting its four kinds with each kind's count, its models when several and its « Activité du prompt » (FR-33, FR-34) (« Pas de consommation dans l’export pour ce tour. » with no usage); the latest run is unfolded at first; once the export holds its 100 runs, subagents included, a note says the oldest ones may be missing, the session's totals staying exact.
- **FR-33** — A run's « Activité du prompt » shows in its unfolded line of « Prompts » (the current run's from the export's freshest copy), or alone under the summary when no run is listed: its tags (`session` BR-19), its 5 most called tools (the advisor included) and its 5 most loaded skills, each with a bar against the most used, its count and, for a skill, how it was loaded (« outil », « commande », « préchargée »), then « + N autres ». With no run listed and no session total, the current run's figures stand in the summary.
- **FR-38** — « Activité de la session », between « Modèles » and « Prompts » when runs are listed, sums each tool and each skill (by how it was loaded) over the runs the export keeps and shows them as in FR-33: the 5 most used of each, with bar, count and the skill's way of loading, then « + N autres »; it is left out when no run called a tool or loaded a skill.
- **FR-34** — A run's « Prompts » lists one line per prompt, oldest first: its time (« — » when unknown), a bar of its tokens scaled to the biggest prompt, its tokens and calls; when the run lists 50 prompts whose calls fall short of the run's and its figures are not partial, a note says only the latest 50 are listed, the run's totals staying exact (the export keeps no count of the prompts it dropped).
- **FR-35** — Token counts read abbreviated (« 34 k », « 1,2 M »), the exact count in the tooltip.
- **FR-31** — A click on a section's title in the detail folds the section to its title (its count still shown) or unfolds it; the folded sections are remembered in the browser, the same for every session, one set for the side panel and one for the full screen. By default the side panel shows « Ce qui est attendu », « Action suivante », « Peut-on fermer la session ? » and « Récap et commits » unfolded and folds « Déroulé du tour », « Fichiers modifiés », « Consommation », « Sous-agents » and « Modèle et permissions »; the full screen folds none. A section with nothing to show beyond its heading (nothing awaited, nothing blocking the close, no subagent, no file modified) is its heading alone, its summary on it, and neither folds nor unfolds.

## Actions

- **FR-20** — « Ouvrir dans Claude Code » opens a session the desktop app started directly in the Claude app (`session` BR-11), with a toast reading only « Ouvert dans Claude »; any other session gets `claude --resume {id}` copied and a toast reminding to run it in the session's folder, named; when the copy is refused, the toast shows the command selected for a manual copy.
- **FR-21** — « Action suivante » is shown only when the export proposes a selo command, its resume prompt, else the first `Next:` line of the run's done footer (FR-48), and nothing is run: in the list, its icon button copies the command as soon as the pointer rests on it, its card showing the command under « Action suivante copiée » (« Action suivante » when the browser refuses the copy), and a click copies it with the toast of a copy; in the detail, its section, right below « Ce qui est attendu », displays the command in full and copies it on « Copier »; on a card, its button displays the command with « Copier ».
- **FR-48** — In the detail, « Action suivante » lists, under the resume prompt when the export gives one, every `Next:` line of the current run's done footer as the export keeps them, in order, each with its summary, its command and « Copier »; a command already listed shows once, in its first place, with the summary a `Next:` line gives it.
- **FR-26** — An archived session offers no « Action suivante ».
- **FR-22** — « Archiver », offered on every session not archived yet, moves the session to « Archivées » with a toast saying where to find it; on a session whose state is neither « Job done » nor « Tour terminé » (`session` BR-07), a confirmation naming the state comes first; on a session with a note (`session` BR-21), whatever its state, the confirmation shows the whole note; on a flagged session (FR-42), whatever its state, a confirmation saying it is marked as important comes first; nothing is archived until « Archiver quand même ».
- **FR-23** — « Désarchiver », in an archived session's detail, puts it back in the list.
- **FR-40** — « Note », on every session (row, card and detail), opens a small dialog « Note sur la session » with one text field holding the current note, a count « 12 / 200 » and no more than 200 characters typed, « Enregistrer » (also on Entrée), « Annuler » (also on Échap) and, when the session has a note, « Effacer »; saving an empty text removes the note (`session` BR-21), and a failed save keeps the dialog open with its error.

## New session

- **FR-44** — The « Nouvelle session » window opens in the mode chosen last (FR-49), « Simple » the first time, and closes on « Fermer »: its top line holds « Dans », which chooses the project among those the export holds (`project` BR-01), its folder shown beside it, and the « Simple » / « Avancé » switch. Switching mode keeps the project, the request and the files it touches.
- **FR-45** — In « Simple », « Que veux-tu faire ? » takes the request, then « Pendant que l'agent travaille » chooses « Je reste là » (pilot: selo picks the lane and asks its questions) or « Je m'absente » (pilot autonome), the one chosen last (FR-49), « Je reste là » the first time; under a « ou » rule, « Reprendre » lists the project's blueprints « Prêt » or « En construction » and its mockups to build (FR-50), each with its state or « Maquette » and « Choisir », and is left out when there is none. A chosen blueprint replaces the request field with its card; a chosen mockup shows its card above the request field, the request filled with the mockup's title when empty; « Écrire une demande » drops the choice and keeps the request. A last line sends to « Avancé » to mock up, review, test or pick the lane.
- **FR-46** — In « Avancé », what to do is a chip among three groups, each named by what it does, with its selo skill in its lane's colour (FR-29): « Faire » — « Confier à l'agent » (pilot autonome, chosen when the window opens), « Laisser selo choisir la voie » (pilot), « Changer quelque chose » (fastlane), « Voir avant de coder » (mockup), « Concevoir une évolution » (blueprint), « Construire un blueprint » (build, with the count of the project's blueprints « Prêt » or « En construction », disabled at 0); « Vérifier » — « Me faire questionner » (grill), « Relire du travail » (review), « Tester dans le navigateur » (try); « Sans skill » — « Session libre »; under them, a line on when to choose the chosen one and « Le guide de choix », which opens the help in a new tab. The form asks for the request (« Demande »; optional for review and try, which then take the current work), except build, which picks one of those blueprints; « Confier à l'agent » starts from a request or from such a blueprint. Every choice that takes a request offers « Ce que ça touche » (facultatif): « Page ou règle du domaine » lists the project's page specs and domain sheets with a search, each one picked becoming a chip in its colour (`app` FR-04) that removes it. Its options are switches named in words with their selo flag beside them: for « Confier à l'agent » from a request, « Lane » (« Au choix de l'agent », « Fastlane », « Blueprint ») and « S'arrêter au blueprint », disabled with « Fastlane »; for fastlane, « Mode itératif »; for fastlane and blueprint, « Partir d'une maquette », with the choice among the project's mockups to build (FR-50); for build, « Une seule étape » with its number; for both pilot choices, fastlane and build, « Sans test navigateur »; the other choices have none.
- **FR-47** — At the window's foot, the command reads as it is typed: in « Simple », `/selo:pilot {request}`, `/selo:build {blueprint}` for a chosen blueprint, `— mockup {file}` after the request for a chosen mockup, and `— autonomous` last with « Je m'absente » (a blueprint then gives `/selo:pilot {blueprint} — autonomous`); in « Avancé », `/selo:{skill}`, then the request or the blueprint's file, then each file it touches as `@{file}`, then each option's flag (`— mockup {file}`, `— no browser`), « Confier à l'agent » ending with `— autonomous`, « Session libre » giving the request and its files alone. Its copy button copies it. « Copier et ouvrir » (also Ctrl+Entrée), disabled until the request or the blueprint is given, copies it, closes the window and opens a new session of the Claude app in the project's folder (for a blueprint or a mockup only a worktree holds, that worktree's, `project` BR-01, the folder shown being that one) with the command already typed in, nothing sent; its toast « Claude Desktop s'ouvre dans {projet} » says to confirm the folder, then press Entrée, and offers « Rouvrir »; a refused copy still opens the session. Below 640 px the window covers the screen in one column, the mode switch under its title and its foot kept at the bottom.
- **FR-49** — The window remembers, in this browser, the mode and the « Je reste là » / « Je m'absente » choice chosen last, so that the next window opens on them.
- **FR-50** — A mockup to build is a file of the project's `.planning/mockups/` that the export marks validated and not committed (the lane that builds a mockup commits it with its work), named by its title.

## Non-goals

- Acting on a session: the page only copies commands, opens a session or a new one in the Claude app, archives, keeps a note and flags; it never sends a prompt.
- Copying the transcript's path, useful only for debugging.
- A coloured chip per project: the project's name is enough.
- For a session outside selo: its end-of-turn phase, commits, blockers, subagents, and any history once its process ended.
- The history of a subagent's run: an autonomous pilot's main run already carries its subagents' skills.
